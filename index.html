01

Project

BETWEEN is a relay traveling across art book fairs in Asia. At each location, a local team selects twenty books from their own shelves within twenty-four hours. The books are passed to the next location, where they are interpreted and exhibited alongside a new selection.

The process is not only documented. It is the project itself.

About

Since the NY Art Book Fair was first held in 2006, art book fairs have fostered distinct communities and publishing practices in the places where they take root.

BETWEEN is a relay traveling across art book fairs in Asia, documenting publishing practices as they continue to evolve across the region.

The art book fair is not only a venue for the project, but also its method.
